## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight at benchmark craft: a white, calm learning product whose proof is the report itself. Refuses the icon-tile card wall.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink (#111827), one blue (#2563EB) for actions, links, the current item and the user's own data marks; grey-50 panels; hairline borders, 6-18px radii, almost no shadow. Be Vietnam Pro throughout (self-hosted), heavy tight headings, tabular numerals. Green/amber/red only for score states.

STORY: Visitor sees what it is in one screen, meets the real report in the solution section, presses Bắt đầu ngay; in the app every screen has one solid blue button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plain top bar (logo left, links, Đăng nhập, blue Bắt đầu ngay). Left column: headline capped at 54px (the kept Vietnamese headline is long; larger wraps to five lines) with "tốt hơn" in blue, lead, primary button plus outline "Xem báo cáo mẫu", facts row. Right column, by the owner's later instruction (2026-11-10, after a reference screenshot; answers: "Only the hero idea" and "The sphere is changed to be the continuous replacement of images about presentation, reporting, ... you can take them everywhere"): a rotating sphere of photo tiles about presenting, reporting, interviews and pitching.
